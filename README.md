# Sanctuary Chorus Listening Bands

A derived dataset of 952 mel-spectrogram images (240 x 64) of 60-second underwater recordings made during logged fish choruses at four hydrophone sites in Channel Islands and Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctuaries (33 deployments, March 2019 to July 2024). Each recording ranks six fixed candidate listening bands by how far the recording stands out above its deployment's chorus-free sound level.

Sources: NOAA Office of National Marine Sanctuaries and U.S. Navy, Sanctuary Soundscape Monitoring Project (SanctSound), and NOAA Office of National Marine Sanctuaries passive acoustic recordings and fish chorus logs, NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (U.S. Government works, public domain).
